    RN / Quick start

    Ready to run the room.

    1. Open the audience screen

      Choose Open presenter, move that window onto the conference display, then click it once for fullscreen and countdown sound. Keep Flow Master on your laptop.

    2. Make it easy to read

      Use Screen size − / + while looking at the actual display. The QR grows too. Fit restores 100%. Check scanning from the back of the room.

    3. Let the room join

      Guests scan the QR or use the short link. Try one response on a real phone before the event. Keep phone screens awake and check the connected count.

    4. Run it with Next

      The small label on Next tells you exactly what happens. Polls open voting automatically when you enter them. Then Next tallies votes → shows results → moves on. Give guests time to answer. To preview polls before voting opens, turn off Open polls automatically in Advanced tools.

    5. Race and review

      For Fill, wait until phones are ready, then Next starts the countdown. The race ends automatically. For Pinboard, Approve the responses you want the room to see.

    Back and Reset restart an activity. Advanced tools contains timer/target settings, Pause, Skip and Start new run. A new run gives a new join code: reopen Presenter and ask guests to rejoin.

    If updates stop: Pause if available, keep the same session open and let devices reconnect. If the venue connection remains unreliable, switch to the downloaded PowerPoint backup.
